VENUS makes the structure of the headphones part of their visual identity. A perforated headband and exposed connecting elements sit above broad, circular earcups with finely lined outer surfaces. Black cushioning adds contrast to the silver exterior and gives the silhouette a clear inner and outer layer. Its open framework and repeated surface details bring a more architectural character to the ECHO headphone collection.
